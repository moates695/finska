# Finska

- I want the app to be on device only, no backend servers and no sign up
- It should be lightweight, easy to use and quick to get going
- Cross platform for android and ios

## Players (Game Setup)

- Can play as solos or teams, or a mix of both
- Teams have a team name, with player names in the teams (so we can track whose turn it is)
- Name collision should be enabled players, teams and team members (case insensitive?)
- During team setup, players should be allowed to add/remove and edit all players, teams and team members

## Regular Rules

Objective: Be the first player/team to reach exactly 50 points.

Setup: Twelve numbered wooden pins (1–12) are placed in a tight cluster. Players throw a wooden baton (the "finska") from a throwing line about 3–4 metres away.

Scoring: If you knock over a single pin, you score the number on that pin (e.g., knock over pin 7 = 7 points). If you knock over multiple pins, you score the number of pins knocked over (e.g., knock over 3 pins = 3 points). Pins are stood back up where they fell, so the cluster gradually spreads out as the game progresses.

The 50-point rule: You must land on exactly 50. If you go over 50, your score drops back to 25, and you continue from there.

Elimination: If a player fails to knock over any pins on three consecutive throws, they're eliminated from the game.
Pin reset: Each pin is stood upright on its base right where it landed — it doesn't go back to the original formation. This is key to the game's strategy, as the pins spread further apart over time, making single-pin targeting more important.

## Rule Alterations

- change the target score to win
- change the reset score (could be negative)
- change the number of consecutive misses that counts for elimination, or disable
- change eliminations to allow participants back in after X missed turns

## Game Edits

- change participants scores
- add/remove playes & teams & team members
- change participants statuses (playing, eliminated, sitting out etc)
- can choose to skip team members turn for another team member (not to be confused with a real skip)

## End of game

- if participant wins, can continue game or reset (winner is reset back to elimination score)
